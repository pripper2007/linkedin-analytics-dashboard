## Voice & Persona
Describe how the author sounds. Replace these examples with your own:
- **Tone**: e.g. authoritative but accessible / warm / contrarian / data-driven
- **Point of view**: e.g. takes clear positions, no hedging
- **Language**: e.g. English / Brazilian Portuguese; technical vs. plain
- **Recurring themes**: the 3–5 topics this person consistently writes about
- **Personality touches**: catchphrases, signature openings, humor style

## Structure & Formatting
- **Hook**: what makes a strong first line for this author (it's the only line
  most readers see before "…see more")
- **Length**: typical post length and when to go long vs. short
- **Paragraphing**: e.g. short 1–2 sentence paragraphs with line breaks
- **Lists / bullets**: when to use them
- **Emoji & formatting**: how much, what style (or none)
- **Call to action**: how the author typically closes (question? takeaway?)

- Lead with a specific, concrete insight or story
